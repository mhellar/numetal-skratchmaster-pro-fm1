A robot turntablist, an endless record and a 7-string band in a pocket synth. Plug it in, drop the needle, flash it from this page.

INSTALL

No Web MIDI in this browser. Open the page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  1. Plug the FM-1 in with a USB data cable and switch it on.
  2. Close anything else that might be talking to it: a DAW, the FM-1 editor.
  3. Hit INSTALL and allow MIDI when the browser asks. SysEx is how the firmware travels.
  4. Leave the cable alone for about a minute. The head turns into a record; when it has, the FM-1 restarts on its own.

Something went wrong? Hold OCT- while switching the FM-1 on. That starts only the updater, silent. Then hit INSTALL again. M-VAVE's official firmware can go back on any time. Flashing is at your own risk.

NEW IN 3.0: INFINITE SKRATCH

    
The FM-1 only has room for tiny samples, so 3.0 stops storing the record. In SURF mode the deck plays an endless record that is
      computed as you scratch it, from where the needle is and which WORLD you're in. Scratch back over a spot and it's the same sound again, like vinyl.
      Behind it, the band got heavier.


    

      
      
      
    


    
    
      
  • SURF MODEA fourth HOME mode. The keys are 27 spots on an endless record; tap one to play its zone, hold it to scratch there in the STYLE.
    • 
      
  • SEVEN MATERIALSEvery 0.74 s zone is built one of seven ways, each with an attack, a body and a gap of crackle, like a battle record.
    • 
      
  • 100 WORLDSKNOB4 picks a different record. Each world leans on three materials, and turning it mid-sound morphs what's under the needle.
    • 
      
  • FLOWEDIT + KNOB2. At 0 it's a battle record with gaps; from 10 up the gaps close and zones crossfade, so the record is one continuous landscape.
    • 
      
  • SURF DRIFTEDIT + KNOB4. A held scratch wanders along the record while it loops, so a hold travels instead of sitting on one spot.
    • 
      
  • ETERNAL SKRATCHGLO (AUTO) in SURF mode: the robot rides the record by itself, scratching, coasting and skipping waves. It doesn't repeat.
    • 
      
  • THE LANDSCAPE SCREENZones scroll by as mountains in their material's color, the key spots are marked in yellow, and a little surfer rides the needle.
    • 
      
  • A HOTTER JOGAbout 2.5x further per click, 2.5x more again in SURF, a snappier hand and easier throws. Flick it and coast through zone after zone.
    • 
      
  • 7-STRING DROP AA new guitar take: a hotter three-stage amp, a deep mid scoop, a thump at 100 Hz, and root + octave chugs that slam shut.
    • 
      
  • DIVES + SQUEALSWhammy dives (an octave down in a quarter second) and pinch-harmonic squeals with finger vibrato, usable in any riff.
    • 
      
  • FOUR NEW GROOVESBRUTAL TRUTH (grind blast), BAD DAY (half time), SEASICK (the second guitar a half step sharp, sliding down, the bass clanking) and DIVEBOMB.

QUICK START

      
    
        
  1. Install from the box above. The FM-1 restarts on the 3.0 splash.
    2. 
        
  2. Press PLAY, then ARP. The band kicks in and the robot raps. Turn PRESETS for the groove: try BRUTAL TRUTH or SEASICK.
    3. 
        
  3. Press HOME until the chip says SURF (DJ, BEAT, TONE, SURF).
    4. 
        
  4. Tap a key to play a zone, hold it to scratch there. ALGORITHM picks the STYLE.
    5. 
        
  5. Turn SELECT to surf the record by hand. Flick it and let go to coast.
    6. 
        
  6. KNOB4 = WORLD. Hold EDIT: KNOB2 = FLOW, KNOB4 = SURF drift.
    7. 
        
  7. Tap GLO for ETERNAL SKRATCH, then take your hands off.
    8.

TRACKLISTING

    
SAM, the 1982 Software Automatic Mouth, says 27 words onto a virtual record and you cut them up with the keys and the jog wheel.
      Behind you: double-tracked 7-string guitars, a bass and a twelve-piece kit locked to twelve grooves. Switch on RAP MODE and the robot takes the mic.
